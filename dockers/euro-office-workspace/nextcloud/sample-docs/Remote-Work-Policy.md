# GreenBrew — Remote Work Policy

_Effective 2025-01-01._

## Eligibility
All salaried employees past their 90-day probation may work remotely up to three
days per week. Roasting and fulfilment roles are on-site by nature.

## Core hours
Remote staff must be reachable 10:00–15:00 Pacific. Outside core hours, schedules
are flexible.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a stipend of USD 300 for home-office setup.
Coffee, naturally, is unlimited and complimentary.

## Security
Company data must only be accessed from company-managed devices over the VPN.
Documents are stored in the shared workspace, never on personal drives.
