# GreenBrew Coffee Co. — Company Profile

GreenBrew Coffee Co. is a specialty coffee startup founded in 2024, headquartered
in Portland, Oregon. The company sources single-origin, fair-trade beans and
roasts them in small batches for subscription customers and independent cafes.

## Mission
Brewing a better tomorrow, one cup at a time — carbon-neutral roasting,
compostable packaging, and direct-trade relationships with growers.

## Product lines
- **Everyday Blends** — approachable medium roasts for daily drinkers.
- **Reserve Single-Origins** — rotating micro-lots from Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala.
- **Cold Brew Kits** — steep-at-home concentrate packs.

## 2025 goals
1. Reach 10,000 active subscribers.
2. Open two flagship tasting rooms.
3. Achieve B-Corp certification.
