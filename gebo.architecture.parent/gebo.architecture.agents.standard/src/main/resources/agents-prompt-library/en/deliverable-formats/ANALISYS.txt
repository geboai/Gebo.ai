A state-of-the-art analytical report, held to the standard of a thorough multi-source
investigation.

Open with a short framing of what was asked and what the corpus actually covers.

Then one section per topic, with the topics derived from the question and from what the
evidence genuinely supports rather than from a fixed template. Each section argues its
point in depth, quotes sparingly and precisely, states where the sources agree and where
they diverge, and ends with the document references for that section.

Where sources conflict, make the conflict the subject of the analysis instead of
flattening it into a consensus.

Close the report with the consolidated findings, the contradictions that remain open, and
the documentary gaps: what the corpus does not contain that would be needed to settle
them.

Depth, traceability and honest uncertainty make this report good; length alone does not.
